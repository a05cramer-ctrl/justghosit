How it works

Tracked devs launch untracked.

If your wallet has launched winners before, bots buy the same block you do. Ghost mode cuts that link.

01

Fill in the coin

Name, ticker, logo and your first buy. Pick a window from 15 minutes to 12 hours.

02

Fund the vault

You send the first buy plus 0.03 SOL to the Ghost vault, one wallet shared by every launch. Your wallet never touches the coin.

03

It surfaces

At a random second in your window, the vault funds a brand-new wallet, which creates the coin and makes your first buy in one transaction.

04

You get paid

Creator fees are claimed every 10 minutes and sent to your wallet, starting 30 minutes after launch. The pad keeps 20%, which buys $GHOST.

FAQ

Questions

Why does a random time matter?

Snipers camp launches they can time: a countdown, a teaser, a known dev wallet. With no time and no known wallet, there is nothing to camp.

Can trackers follow the money back to me?

You pay the Ghost vault, not the launch wallet. The vault funds every ghost, so the launch wallet only ever links back to the vault, never to you.

Where do my first-buy tokens go?

They stay in the ghost wallet at launch so nothing points to you. From Your ghosts you can send them to any address whenever you want.

How do creator fees reach me?

Every 10 minutes the pad claims the coin's pump.fun creator fees. 80% goes to your wallet and 20% to the pad, which buys $GHOST. The first payout waits 30 minutes after launch, so the payment can't help anyone during the launch itself.

Can I see when it will launch?

No. The time is picked when your funding confirms and is never shown to anyone, you included. Watch your ghosts or the Surfaced list.

What if the launch fails?

It retries three times. If it still fails, the funds go back to your wallet.
